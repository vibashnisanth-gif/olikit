# Olikit Product Foundation

> This document is the permanent foundation of Olikit. It defines what we are, what we are not, and how every decision should be made. It is immutable unless evidence proves it should change. Every designer, engineer, and AI coding agent must read and obey this document before making any change to the product.

---

## 1. Mission

Help every person maximize their lifetime earning potential through personalized career intelligence.

---

## 2. Vision

### Olikit is not:

- A salary website
- A career blog
- A calculator website
- A job board

### Olikit is:

The AI-powered Career Intelligence Platform.

---

## 3. The One Question

Everything Olikit builds must answer:

**"How can I increase my earning potential?"**

Every feature must strengthen this promise. If a feature does not answer this question, it does not belong in Olikit.

---

## 4. Product Principles

These are permanent rules. They do not change with trends.

1. **Interactive before static.** A calculator beats a table. A tool beats an article. If a user can do something instead of read about it, let them do it.

2. **Discovery before reading.** Surface the answer first. Let users dig deeper if they want. Never force reading to find the point.

3. **Data before content.** Numbers are the product. Words support the numbers. If the data is not compelling, the writing will not save it.

4. **Recommendations instead of dead ends.** Every page must answer "what should I do next?" A page that sends a user away is a failed page.

5. **Every page leads somewhere.** No orphan pages. No dead ends. Every URL must connect to at least three other relevant URLs through internal links, recommendations, or navigation.

6. **Personalization without friction.** Learn from the user without asking for information. Use context (location, currency, profession) to tailor the experience silently.

7. **Build trust before monetization.** Earn attention with quality. Monetize only after the user has received value. Ads must never compromise the experience.

8. **Every interaction should teach something useful.** A user who completes a calculation should know something they did not know before. A user who finishes a page should have gained actionable knowledge.

9. **Every dataset should power multiple experiences.** A salary dataset should power calculators, comparisons, rankings, research, and AI answers. Build once, surface everywhere.

10. **Simplicity beats feature overload.** One feature done well is worth ten features done poorly. Complexity is the enemy of trust.

---

## 5. User Promise

Every visitor to Olikit should leave knowing at least one action they can take to improve their career.

Not "thinking about it." Not "considering it." One concrete, actionable step.

If a user cannot identify what to do next, we have failed.

## 6. Success Metrics

### Primary

| Metric | Definition | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Journey Completion Rate** | % of users who complete a full calculation or comparison | Measures whether we deliver on our promise |
| **Pages per Session** | Average number of pages viewed per visit | Measures engagement and internal link effectiveness |
| **Return Visitors** | % of users who return within 30 days | Measures long-term value and trust |
| **Tool Usage** | Number of calculator/completion sessions per day | Measures product utility |
| **Recommendation Click Rate** | % of users who click a "next step" recommendation | Measures whether we successfully guide users |
| **Organic Traffic** | Monthly unique visitors from search | Measures authority and discoverability |
| **Revenue** | Monthly revenue from ads, affiliates, and tools | Measures business sustainability |

### Secondary

| Metric | Definition | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Session Duration** | Average time spent on site | Measures content depth and engagement |
| **Saved Journeys** | Number of users who save/export calculations | Measures perceived value |
| **AI Citations** | Number of times Olikit is cited by AI assistants | Measures authority in the AI era |

## 7. Product Pillars

Olikit is built on five pillars. Every feature must belong to at least one.

### Career Intelligence

Help users understand which careers pay the most, grow the fastest, and offer the best long-term earning potential.

- Profession salary data across countries
- Career growth trajectories
- Skill premiums and specialization value
- Market demand and automation risk

### Salary Intelligence

Help users understand what they should earn and how to maximize take-home pay.

- Salary calculators by profession, location, and experience
- Tax-adjusted and PPP-adjusted comparisons
- Take-home pay vs gross salary analysis
- Salary benchmarking across markets

### Wealth Intelligence

Help users understand how to build wealth from their income.

- Cost of living comparisons
- Purchasing power analysis
- Savings potential by location
- Investment and retirement planning context

### Decision Intelligence

Help users make better career and relocation decisions.

- Country comparisons for relocation
- Job offer evaluation tools
- Career pivot analysis
- Tax optimization strategies

### AI Career Intelligence

Help users get AI-powered answers to career questions.

- Personalized salary recommendations
- Career path suggestions
- Relocation scoring
- Market trend analysis

---

## 8. Product Rule

Every new feature must answer **YES** to at least four of these five questions:

| # | Question | What It Tests |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does it help users earn more? | User value |
| 2 | Does it strengthen proprietary data? | Competitive moat |
| 3 | Does it improve engagement? | Retention |
| 4 | Does it naturally lead to another discovery? | Internal linking, journey continuity |
| 5 | Will it still be valuable in five years? | Long-term viability |

If a feature does not answer YES to at least four questions, it should not be built.

No exceptions.
