### HARO Strategy

**Response Cadence**: 2-3 responses/day using templates from `reports/haro-outreach-templates.md`

**Priority Topics**:
- Salary comparisons
- Tax planning
- Cost of living
- Remote work compensation
- Career decisions

### Outreach Sequence

1. **Week 1-2**: Publish flagship reports
2. **Week 3-4**: HARO outreach (2-3/day)
3. **Week 5-6**: Direct pitches to Tier 1 publications
4. **Week 7-8**: Direct pitches to Tier 2 publications
5. **Ongoing**: HARO + community participation
