## Olikit's Unique Position

**What competitors don't have**:
1. **Multi-country salary + tax + cost of living in one tool** — PayScale/Salary.com are US-only
2. **Transparent methodology with government sources** — most competitors use crowd-sourced or proprietary data
3. **Client-side calculations** — no data sent to servers (privacy advantage)
4. **PPP-adjusted comparisons** — not available on most salary sites
5. **21 professions × 7 countries = 147 data points** — depth competitors lack
6. **5 proprietary scoring systems** — unique analytical framework

**What competitors do better**:
1. **Scale** — millions of pages vs Olikit's 2,340
2. **User-generated content** — Glassdoor reviews, Numbeo crowd-sourced data
3. **Job integration** — Indeed, Glassdoor connect salary to jobs
4. **Brand recognition** — established trust
5. **Backlink authority** — DA 63-92 vs Olikit's new domain

## Strategic Recommendations

### 1. Double Down on Unique Advantages
- **Multi-country + tax-adjusted** — this is Olikit's core differentiator
- **Transparent methodology** — trust advantage over crowd-sourced data
- **Privacy-first** — client-side calculations as marketing angle

### 2. Create What Competitors Have (Selectively)
- **Flagship annual reports** — PayScale and Salary.com publish these; Olikit needs one
- **Downloadable data** — journalists and researchers want CSV/PDF exports
- **Historical trends** — year-over-year comparisons add depth

### 3. Don't Compete Where Competitors Win
- **Don't try to match Numbeo's 50K cities** — Olikit's 35 cities with salary data is more valuable
- **Don't add job listings** — that's Glassdoor/Indeed territory
- **Don't add user reviews** — that's Glassdoor territory

### 4. Create New Categories
- **Tax-adjusted salary comparison** — no competitor does this well
- **PPP salary analysis** — unique angle
- **Relocation scoring** — combining salary + tax + cost of living + quality of life
- **Career ROI analysis** — salary vs education investment by country
