# Decision Log

**Purpose:** Record all significant product/engineering decisions with rationale.

---

## Format

Each entry:

```
### {date} — {decision title}

**Decision:** {what was decided}
**Rationale:** {why this decision was made}
**Evidence:** {evidence that supports the decision}
**Alternatives considered:** {other options and why they were rejected}
**Reversible:** {yes/no}
**Review date:** {when to revisit this decision}
```
